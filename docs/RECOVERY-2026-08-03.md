# Repository reconstruction, 3 August 2026

## What happened

The repository at `/Users/abhimanyusingh/Downloads/My projects/Jeeves` disappeared
during a working session — the whole `My projects` directory ceased to exist while
`Downloads` was being reorganised into `Work - Jeeves` and `Work - Product Docs`.
It was not in `~/.Trash`, and a Spotlight search for `Jeeves.xcodeproj` returned
nothing anywhere on the disk.

At the time, work was in progress in a **linked git worktree** under the session
scratchpad. A linked worktree keeps its `.git` as a *pointer file* to the parent
repository, so its working files survived intact while every commit object went
with the parent. The worktree was archived to
`~/Downloads/Work - Jeeves/travel-correctness-worktree.tar.gz` and this tree was
restored from it.

## The three lost commits, verbatim

### 1 — `4cd81aa` Six things the trip model was guessing, and now says out loud

Walking five real ingestion scenarios against HEAD — a calendar stay with no
flights, two bare place-windows, an all-day pair joined later by a ticket, two
anchors and nothing else, and a Kabini/Bandipur driving trip — found six places
where the app produced a plausible number instead of admitting it had none.

* A multi-day block with no address now proposes a trip. Every detection rule
  gated on `hasLocation`, and the two journey-length rules need a measurement
  that needs an address, so "Bali 4-11 Sep" typed as a bare title reached none
  of them: eight days got filled with the Bengaluru routine and notified for.
  Weak, because that is also how people record a sprint.

* A stay's end had two meanings. `resolveOverlaps` truncates an earlier stay to
  the day before the next begins — a last night — while the final stay keeps
  whatever the calendar gave, usually a check-out date. Any nights count was
  wrong for one of them. `endIsUnsettled` marks which, and `nightsRange` returns
  "3 or 4" rather than picking the tidier one.

* "Home" was an address. With no saved home, two sites POSTed the literal word
  to Routes and rendered whatever came back as a MEASURED leave-by.

* Every stay-to-stay move was a drive. Right for Kabini to Bandipur, wrong for
  Bali to Singapore, from inputs that look identical. The signal that separates
  them was being discarded: the field mask asked for duration only, so an
  eleven-hour route and no route at all were both just "no minutes". It now
  asks for distance too, and `modeIsAssumed` carries the guess until a road
  answer settles it.

* An imported flight left four and a half hours early. The ticket says check-in
  OPENS 4h before; that was stored as a cut-off, putting you at the terminal at
  18:35 for a 23:05 departure. Opens is not a deadline.

* A sixteen-day-old traffic forecast looked like a live reading.
  `travelIsEstimated` was a bare Bool. `measuredAt` plus a three-valued
  freshness fixes that, and `record()` is now the only way in — five sites set
  the fields by hand, and adding a timestamp to four of them would have been
  worse than adding it to none.

### 2 — `839b6f8` Wire the classifier, so the assumed drive can actually be refuted

`TransferMode` shipped in the previous commit with zero call sites — exercised by
its tests and reached by nothing, which is the same defect as a status line
nobody displays. It is now the thing that decides whether the assumed drive
survives first contact with a road.

`commuteRoute` keeps the distance the field mask now returns; `commuteMinutes`
delegates to it, so nothing that only wanted minutes had to change. When the
verdict is anything but a plausible drive the segment is left unmeasured and
still marked assumed, and the refusal is logged — previously `.drive` simply
stood with no minutes behind it and nothing said so.

### 3 — `601f6ec` What the fork review found, including the regression I shipped

34 of 37 findings confirmed by an adversarial pass over the branch. Two were
blockers and both were mine.

* THE REGRESSION. Relaxing `acceptTravel`'s filter to admit any address-less
  multi-day event let "Sprint review 8-9 Sep" become a stay: `resolveOverlaps`
  truncated Bali to the 7th and bounds shrank the trip to 4-9, so the 10th and
  11th were refilled with the Bengaluru routine and notified for — the exact
  harm the change exists to prevent, reintroduced one line above the fix. It
  also wrote a stay called "Sprint review" and a drive to an empty address
  whose card read "Leave ~11:40". Now only the block the banner was about is
  admitted.

* The detection rule fired on a daily standup. `spanDays` is inferred across
  every same-titled row in the store, so five days of a recurring meeting came
  through as a five-day trip, and a 21:00-01:00 party as a two-day one. The
  rule now needs the row's OWN span and all-day.

* `freshness()` could never return `.predicted` for a drive. Every drive the app
  creates leaves `departAt` at `.distantPast` and carries an `arriveBy` instead —
  and the test passed because its fixture was flight-shaped, a segment nothing
  in the app produces. That is a test that proved nothing about the path it
  claimed to cover. `journeyInstant` resolves the anchor the way `day` already
  does, and the drive case is now pinned with the shape `acceptTravel` really
  makes.

* "Two sites" was wrong: the literal "Home" still reached Routes from the
  segment editor (which measures on open), from chat's commute estimate, and
  from `add_journey`. All three now refuse.

* A too-far road answer discarded a measurement main used to store, trading a
  silent wrong answer for a silent missing one. `record(minutes:settlesMode:)`
  keeps the minutes and keeps the doubt.

* Two address-less stays produced no journey at all: the transition guard read
  `from.address != to.address`, which is false when both are blank — so the
  Bali → Singapore move the refutation was built for existed nowhere.
